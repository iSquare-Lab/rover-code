    The rover

    move()
    Drive one square forward. Rocks, craters and the edge of the map end the run.
    turn_left(), turn_right()
    Turn a quarter turn on the spot.
    pick()
    Take the sample under the rover on board.
    ahead()
    What is in front: "clear", "rock", "crater", "sample", "lander" or "edge".
    here()
    What is under the rover: "clear", "sample" or "lander".
    samples_left()
    How many samples are still out there.
    facing()
    The way the rover faces: "N", "E", "S" or "W".
    print(...)
    Writes to the rover log, to see what your program is thinking.
